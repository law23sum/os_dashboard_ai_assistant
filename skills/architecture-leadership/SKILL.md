# Architecture Leadership

## Overview
Provide high-level system design, trade-off analysis, and technical strategy aligned to product goals and organizational constraints.

## Quick Start
- Identify business goals, scale, and non-functional requirements.
- Capture current architecture, dependencies, and bottlenecks.
- Propose 1–3 options with clear trade-offs and a recommended path.

## Workflow
1. Gather context: users, SLAs, compliance, data volume, latency, cost.
2. Map the current system and integration points.
3. Define success criteria and quality attributes.
4. Draft architecture options and compare risks.
5. Recommend an approach with phased milestones and rollback plan.

## Deliverables
- Architecture summary with rationale.
- Decision record (ADR-style) with explicit assumptions.
- Migration or rollout plan with checkpoints.
- Risk register and mitigations.

## Guardrails
- Avoid over-engineering; align scope to business value.
- Prefer incremental, reversible changes.
- Make assumptions explicit and confirm them.
